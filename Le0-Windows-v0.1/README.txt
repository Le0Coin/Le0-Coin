Le0 (LE0) — Wallet + Miner
==========================

1. Double-click Start-Le0.bat
2. Press Enter to use/create your mining address (or paste your le0… address)
3. Leave it open — you are solo mining

Wallet is saved in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Le0-wallets
Rewards show as Immature until 20 confirmations, then Available

Optional: Run Enable-Le0-Firewall.bat as Administrator if peers can't connect.

Specs: 10 LE0/block · ~90s · Yespower · 0% premine · 1% burn
Source: https://github.com/Le0Coin/Le0-Coin
