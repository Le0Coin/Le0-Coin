Le0 Wallet (no mining)
======================
For people who only want to buy, receive, send, or hold LE0.

1. Unzip.
2. Run Start-Le0-Wallet.bat
3. Create / sign in to your account. Save your 12-word seed.
4. Use Receive to get your address, or Send to move coins.

This pack never turns mining on.
If you want to mine later, download the separate Miner pack (same account works).

Wallet files stay in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Le0-wallets
Only download from the official Le0 site or Discord.
